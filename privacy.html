Contact & privacy

Contact

Cave Dev Software is a software development company based in the Azores, Portugal. For inquiries or questions about your information, email cavdevsoftware@gmail.com.

Email inquiries

This website has no contact form and does not collect inquiry messages. The email link opens your own email application. If you send a message, your email service handles sending it and Gmail handles the receiving inbox.

Your correspondence is used to reply to your inquiry and discuss possible work. Sending an email does not subscribe you to marketing messages. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Website services

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages, and Google Fonts loads the website’s typography. Google Tag Manager loads scripts configured by Cave Dev Software. Google may receive technical information when your browser requests the Tag Manager container. Any additional data collection or cookies depend on the tags enabled in that container and the services they use. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s processing.

Your information

Contact the email address above to request access to, correction of, or deletion of correspondence. Inquiry information is kept only as needed for the conversation and any resulting work or applicable record-keeping requirements.
